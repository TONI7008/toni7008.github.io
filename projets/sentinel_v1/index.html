UQO · GEN 1973 · Student prototype

Remove the threat
from a safe distance.

A tracked bomb-disposal robot with a five-axis arm and a printed gripper. It picks up a suspicious package and sets it down in a safe place, while the operator drives it with bare hands in front of a webcam. Until the hardware is ready, the arm runs in a MATLAB Simscape Multibody simulation.

01 · Mobility

Tracks and power

A two-deck Hiwonder chassis, 193 × 280 mm, with 12 V geared motors and encoders. A 3S 5,200 mAh LiPo powers the tracks and servos for an estimated two and a half hours of testing.

  • Tracked chassis
  • 12 V motors with encoders
  • 3S LiPo · 5,200 mAh

02 · Pick-up

An arm and a gripper

Four DS3225 digital servos (25 kg·cm, metal gears) move the base, shoulder, elbow and wrist pitch. A spring carries part of the shoulder load. An MG90S rolls the wrist, and the kit's MG996R closes a printed parallel gripper with TPU pads.

  • J1–J4: DS3225
  • J5 roll: MG90S · J6 grip: MG996R
  • Jerk-limited motion at 50 Hz

03 · Vision

Two cameras and a lamp

A Camera Module 3 under the deck looks down the road while you drive. At the wrist, a 102° Camera Module 3 Wide and a LUXEON LED lamp sit side by side, tilted so the camera looks at the centre of the grip.

  • Driving camera · 66°
  • Wrist camera · 102°
  • LUXEON LED · 700 mA

04 · Control

Gesture control

MediaPipe tracks both hands in a 60 fps webcam feed. Pinch to move the arm, spread a V sign to work the jaws, and raise an open palm with the other hand to stop all motion.

  • Pinch = move · V sign = jaws
  • Open palm = STOP
  • Commands over Wi-Fi HaLow

05 · Delivery

Set it down somewhere safe

The robot carries the parcel with the arm raised, drives to the drop zone and lowers it until the box rests on the mat. The gripper opens, and the arm folds back for the trip home.

  • Box 50 × 50 × 70 mm · 100 g
  • Carried 140 mm off the ground
  • Up to 159 g at full reach
