Home/Projects/Sentinel V1

UQO · GEN 1973 · Student prototype

Cut the wire
from a safe distance.

Sentinel V1 is a tracked bomb-disposal robot with a six-axis arm and a wire cutter. The operator drives it with bare hands in front of a webcam. Until the hardware is ready, the whole control chain runs against a physics simulator.

01 · Mobility

Tracks and power

A two-deck Hiwonder chassis, 193 × 280 mm, with 12 V geared motors and encoders. A 3S 5,200 mAh LiPo powers the tracks and servos for an estimated two and a half hours of testing.

  • Tracked chassis
  • 12 V motors with encoders
  • 3S LiPo · 5,200 mAh

02 · Manipulation

A six-axis arm

Four DS3225 digital servos (25 kg·cm, metal gears) move the base, shoulder, elbow and wrist pitch. A spring carries part of the shoulder load. Two MG90S servos roll the wrist and close a Hakko CHP-170 flush cutter.

  • J1–J4: DS3225
  • J5 roll, J6 cutter: MG90S
  • Jerk-limited motion at 50 Hz

03 · Vision

Cameras and a lamp

A 102° Camera Module 3 Wide and a LUXEON LED lamp sit side by side at the wrist, tilted toward the jaws of the cutter. A second camera under the deck shows what is ahead of the robot while you drive.

  • Wrist camera · 102°
  • Driving camera · 66°
  • LUXEON LED · 700 mA

04 · Control

Gesture control

MediaPipe tracks both hands in a 60 fps webcam feed. Pinch to move the arm, spread a V sign to work the jaws, and raise an open palm with the other hand to stop all motion.

  • Pinch = move · V sign = jaws
  • Open palm = STOP
  • Commands over Wi-Fi HaLow
