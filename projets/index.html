Watch The Baby

Infant monitor built to help prevent SIDS

A monitor for newborns up to one month old. Six sensors track heart rate and SpO₂, body temperature, room temperature and humidity, motion, crying, and gas or smoke. It also drives a ventilation fan and a buzzer for audible alerts.

The DE1-SoC FPGA handles real-time sensor processing and talks to an ESP32 over UART. The ESP32 reads the biometric sensors and uploads to Firebase, and the caregiver's phone app raises an alert when a reading goes out of range.

PartsDHT11, KY-037, HC-SR501, MQ-2, MAX30102, MLX90614, fan, buzzer

Desktop apps

  • C++17 · Qt 6 · CMake

    Checkers (Jeu de Dames)

    International draughts on a 10×10 board, with forced and chain captures and king promotion. The AI runs minimax with alpha-beta pruning on a background thread, at difficulty levels from 1 to 9. Two people can also play over a LAN. The game finds opponents by UDP broadcast and sends moves over TCP.

    Source on GitHub

  • C++ · Qt 6 · CMake

    Connect 4

    Connect 4 against an alpha-beta AI. A slider sets its search depth from 2 to 8, and it takes immediate wins, blocks yours and favors the center column. You can switch the AI on halfway through a two-player game. At launch, the app checks GitHub Releases and downloads the installer for your platform when a newer version is out.

    Source on GitHub

In your browser

  • Game · JavaScript

    Minesweeper

    Three board sizes: 9×9 with 10 mines, 16×16 with 40, and 30×16 with 99. Mines go down after your first click, never on that cell or the eight around it, so every game opens with a clear patch. Right-click or long-press to flag.

    Play Source

  • Numerical methods · JavaScript

    Newton's method solver

    Type f(x) and a starting guess. math.js computes the symbolic derivative, KaTeX typesets both, and Chart.js plots the step size at each iteration. Export the run as CSV when you're done.

    Open solver Source

  • LLM client · JavaScript

    Local AI chat

    A chat window for language models on your own machine. It connects to any OpenAI-compatible server, such as LM Studio, Ollama or llama.cpp, streams replies, renders Markdown and sends images to vision models. Start a local server before you open it.

    Open chat Source
