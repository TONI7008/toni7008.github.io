Watch The Baby

Infant monitor built to help prevent SIDS

A monitor for newborns up to one month old. Six sensors track heart rate and SpO₂, body temperature, room temperature and humidity, motion, crying, and gas or smoke.

The DE1-SoC FPGA handles real-time sensor processing and talks to an ESP32 over UART. The ESP32 reads the biometric sensors and uploads to Firebase, and the caregiver's phone app raises an alert when a reading goes out of range.

PartsDHT11, KY-037, HC-SR501, MQ-2, MAX30102, MLX90614, fan, buzzer

    Checkers (Jeu de Dames)

    International draughts on a 10×10 board, with forced and chain captures and king promotion. The AI runs minimax with alpha-beta pruning on a background thread, at difficulty levels from 1 to 9. Two people can also play over a LAN. The game finds opponents by UDP broadcast and sends moves over TCP.

    Source on GitHub

  • Desktop · C++ / Qt 6

    Connect 4

    Connect 4 against an alpha-beta AI. A slider sets its search depth from 2 to 8, and it takes immediate wins, blocks yours and favors the center column. At launch, the app checks GitHub Releases and downloads the installer for your platform when a newer version is out.

    Source on GitHub

Three more run in this browser: Minesweeper, a Newton's method solver and a chat client for local LLMs.
