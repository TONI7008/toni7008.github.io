Sentinel V1

Bomb-disposal robot driven by hand gestures (GEN 1973, UQO)

A tracked robot with a six-axis arm and a wire cutter. The operator drives it with bare hands in front of a webcam. MediaPipe tracks both hands: a pinch moves the arm, a V sign works the cutter, and an open palm from the other hand stops everything. A game controller works too.

The station sends commands over UDP to a Raspberry Pi 5, which runs inverse kinematics and jerk-limited trajectories in Rust at 50 Hz. An Arduino Mega drives the servos and tracks. While the hardware is being built, the same chain runs against a PyBullet simulator, and a parity test checks the Rust code against the Python simulator.

Watch The Baby

Infant monitor built to help prevent SIDS

A monitor for newborns up to one month old. Six sensors track heart rate and SpO₂, body temperature, room temperature and humidity, motion, crying, and gas or smoke.

The DE1-SoC FPGA handles real-time sensor processing and talks to an ESP32 over UART. The ESP32 reads the biometric sensors and uploads to Firebase, and the caregiver's phone app raises an alert when a reading goes out of range.

PartsDHT11, KY-037, HC-SR501, MQ-2, MAX30102, MLX90614, fan, buzzer
